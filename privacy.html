Manifestle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Manifestle is a private ritual app: you seal intentions, talk with Luna, record daily check-ins, and keep a dream journal. This policy describes exactly what stays on your device, what is sent elsewhere, when, and why. Where this policy and the app disagree, treat it as a bug and tell us at privacy@tortubuild.com.

Manifestle is operated by TortuBuild ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • Without an account, your manifests, chat history, and daily check-ins are stored only on your device.
  • Luna's replies are generated by AI. They require your explicit in-app permission. Until you grant it, nothing you write is transmitted.
  • If you link an Apple or Google account, your manifests (including their conversations with Luna), daily check-ins, dream journal, and Luna profile settings are stored in our cloud so they can be restored on a new or reinstalled device.
  • The Dream Journal needs an account. Writing and saving a dream sends it nowhere; a dream is only ever sent to be interpreted when you ask Luna to read that particular one.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app.

2. Data stored only on your device

Before you link an account, and for as long as you remain a guest ("wanderer"), the following never leaves your device:

  • Your sealed manifests and their text.
  • Your conversations with Luna.
  • Your daily check-ins, moods, and notes.
  • Your Luna companion progress, appearance choices, reminder time, appearance and language preferences.
  • The birth date and zodiac sign you choose when you first open the app, and the crystal ball readings Luna gives you that day.
  • How much of today's free allowance you have used — the messages Luna has answered, the crystal ball readings she has given, and the dreams she has read. This is counted per device, never uploaded, and it resets each day.

Deleting the app deletes this local data. Note that a linked account's copy in our cloud is not deleted by removing the app — see section 8.

3. Luna's AI replies

Luna's replies are written by Google Gemini. Manifestle asks for your explicit permission the first time Luna would reply, and never transmits anything before you grant it. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Luna's AI Replies; the app stops sending immediately.

When permission is granted, each reply sends the following to our own server, which relays it to the Google Gemini API:

  • The message you just wrote.
  • The text of the intention Luna is holding for that conversation.
  • Up to the last 10 messages of that conversation.
  • Your app language and the Luna persona you chose.

Luna's crystal ball readings are written the same way, under the same permission. Opening a reading sends only your zodiac sign, whether the reading is daily or weekly, the calendar date it is for, your app language, and the Luna persona you chose. Your birth date itself is never sent — only the sign — and no manifest, message, or check-in is included.

Luna's dream interpretations are written the same way, under the same permission, and only when you ask her to read a specific dream. Writing a dream down and saving it sends it nowhere. When you do ask her to read one, we send the text of that single dream, the calendar date it was written on, your app language, and the Luna persona you chose. No other dream, and no manifest, message, or check-in, is included.

Your name, email address, and account identity are not included in what we send to Google. Requests are authenticated so we can enforce abuse limits, but the authenticated identifier is used by our server only and is not forwarded as part of the prompt.

Training and retention. We use the Google Gemini API under a paid service tier, under which Google states that it does not use submitted prompts or responses to train its models. Google processes and retains request data according to its own terms; see the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. On our own servers, the text of a Luna reply, reading, or dream interpretation is stored briefly so that a retried request cannot be charged or answered twice; these records are automatically deleted after 30 days, and immediately when you delete your account.

Luna is a companion, not a therapist, doctor, or emergency service. If a message suggests immediate risk of harm, the app responds with crisis resources instead of a companion reply.

5. Shared Manifest and other people

Shared Manifest lets you share a journey with a friend. Anything you post there — your journey title, daily entries, and notes — is visible to the member you share it with. Do not put anything in a shared journey that you would not want that person to read.

Every shared journey offers a Report control for content that is abusive or otherwise objectionable, alongside the ability to remove the friend. Reports reach us at support@tortubuild.com and we act on them, including removing content and terminating accounts.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers. The app does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track you.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not use your journal content for advertising or profiling.

7. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check) — account sign-in, cloud storage of the data listed in section 4, notifications, and abuse prevention.
  • Google Gemini API — generating Luna's replies, as described in section 3.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and App Store purchases.

8. Deleting your data

Settings → Your Data → Delete Manifestle Account deletes your account and the data stored with it: your cloud manifests and conversations, daily check-ins, your dream journal and Luna's interpretations of those dreams, Luna profile state, your birth date and zodiac sign, username reservation, avatar, persona, friend invitations and friendships, your entries in shared journeys, your notification tokens, and your Luna chat records. The deletion is permanent and immediate; there is no recovery period and no archived copy.

It also clears everything the app holds about you on that device — your manifests and conversations, check-ins, dreams and their interpretations, Luna progress, birth date and zodiac sign, avatar and persona, preferences, the app's offline cache of your cloud data, and your AI permission — and removes your sign-in. If your device cannot reach us, nothing is deleted anywhere and the app tells you so, so that your journey is never cleared from the device while the cloud copy survives.

Starting again afterwards starts you from nothing: a new account is a new account, and no manifest, profile picture, birth date, preference, or progress from the deleted one comes back — with the single exception described next.

What the device keeps. Manifestle gives each device a small free allowance every day: how many messages Luna will answer, how many crystal ball readings she will give, and how many dreams she will read. How much of today's allowance you have already spent is a count held on the device. It is not part of your account — it contains nothing you wrote and nothing Luna said, it is never uploaded, and it is never shown to anyone. Deleting your account, logging out, and creating a new account all leave that count exactly as it is, and it resets on its own when the next day begins. We do it this way so the day's free allowance cannot be refilled by deleting an account and making another.

What we keep. If you reported someone's content to us, that report — including the fact that you filed it — is kept after your account is deleted, because we need it to act on abuse and to show we did. Anything a friend independently holds, such as a shared journey you both took part in, stays with their account; your entries and your membership are removed from it.

Deleting the app alone does not delete the cloud copy, because your sign-in survives on the device. Use Delete Manifestle Account, or write to privacy@tortubuild.com and we will delete it for you.

You can also export your manifests and reflections at any time from Settings → Your Data → Export My Data.

9. Children

Manifestle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. The in-app export and delete controls cover most of these directly. For anything else, write to privacy@tortubuild.com.

11. Changes

If we change how Manifestle handles your data, we will update this page and its date. Material changes to what leaves your device will also be surfaced in the app.
