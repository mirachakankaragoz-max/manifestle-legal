Manifestle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Manifestle is a private ritual app: you seal intentions, talk with Luna, record daily check-ins, keep a dream journal, and open a fortune cookie. This policy describes exactly what stays on your device, what is sent elsewhere, when, and why. Where this policy and the app disagree, treat it as a bug and tell us at privacy@tortubuild.com.

Manifestle is operated by TortuBuild ("we", "us"), which is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact the controller at privacy@tortubuild.com.

1. The short version

  • Without an account, your manifests, chat history, and daily check-ins are stored only on your device.
  • Luna's replies are generated by AI. They require your explicit in-app permission. Until you grant it, nothing you write is sent to OpenAI for Luna's AI features.
  • If you link an Apple or Google account, your manifests (including their conversations with Luna), daily check-ins, dream journal, and Luna profile settings are stored in our cloud so they can be restored on a new or reinstalled device.
  • Luna Memory is a Manifestle Plus feature for linked accounts. It keeps a compact set of useful details from your conversations. You can view or clear it in Settings.
  • For a linked account, we also store the current day's used Crystal Ball, dream interpretation, and fortune-cookie allowances. These usage markers contain no text you wrote and no reading, interpretation, or fortune Luna generated. They prevent today's allowances from being restored by logging out, reinstalling, or signing in elsewhere.
  • The Dream Journal needs an account. Writing and saving a dream sends it nowhere; a dream is only ever sent to be interpreted when you ask Luna to read that particular one.
  • The fortune cookie needs an account too, and sends nothing you have written. Breaking one asks Luna for a line; the request carries the date, your app language, which of the day's cookies it is, and the Luna persona you chose. It does not include anything you wrote, and it does not include your zodiac sign.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app.

2. Data stored only on your device

Before you link an account, and for as long as you remain a guest ("wanderer"), the following never leaves your device:

  • Your sealed manifests and their text.
  • Your conversations with Luna.
  • Your daily check-ins, moods, and notes.
  • Your Luna companion progress, appearance choices, reminder time, appearance and language preferences.
  • The birth date and zodiac sign you choose when you first open the app, the crystal ball readings Luna gives you that day, and the fortunes she writes in that day's cookies.
  • While you remain a guest, how much of today's free allowance you have used — the messages Luna has answered, the crystal ball readings she has given, the dreams she has read, and the fortune cookies you have opened. These guest counts are kept on that device, are not uploaded, and reset each day. If you link an account, the limited charm usage described in section 4 is also stored under that account.

Deleting the app removes this local data from the device. Note that a linked account's copy in our cloud is not deleted by removing the app — see section 9. On iOS, a separate system backup may retain a copy as described below.

Your phone's own backup. "Never leaves your device" means Manifestle does not send it. Manifestle disables Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer for all local app data, so Android does not copy the information listed above into your Google backup or transfer it to a replacement phone. On iOS, an iCloud or computer backup may include local app data and may put it back when you restore your phone. iOS backups are made by Apple under your Apple account and are governed by Apple's terms rather than by this policy; we cannot read them and we are not sent a copy.

Because Android backup is disabled, a guest journey cannot be recovered after the app is uninstalled or the phone is lost or replaced. A linked account can restore the Local Journey data described in section 4 from Manifestle's cloud.

3. Luna's AI replies

Luna's replies are written using the OpenAI API. Manifestle asks for your explicit permission the first time an AI feature would send data to OpenAI. Until you grant it, Manifestle does not send any of the data described in this section to OpenAI. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Luna's AI Replies; the app stops new AI requests immediately.

When permission is granted, each reply sends the following to our own server, which relays it to the OpenAI API:

  • The message you just wrote.
  • The text of the intention Luna is holding for that conversation.
  • Up to the last 10 messages of that conversation.
  • For a linked account with active Manifestle Plus, Luna's compact memory from earlier conversations, if one exists.
  • Your app language and the Luna persona you chose.

Luna Memory. For a linked account with active Manifestle Plus, our server may periodically ask OpenAI to update a compact memory using the recent conversation. It is limited to useful details such as preferences, important people, goals, routines, and milestones. We instruct the model not to infer or retain sensitive traits, credentials, precise location, financial details, diagnoses, or transient moods. The resulting memory is stored privately in Firebase under your account, is included only in future Luna chats while Plus is active, and is never visible to Shared Manifest friends. If Plus expires, the memory is paused but remains available for you to view or clear in Settings → Luna's Memory.

Luna's crystal ball readings are written the same way, under the same permission. Opening a reading sends only your zodiac sign, whether the reading is daily or weekly, the calendar date it is for, your app language, and the Luna persona you chose. Your birth date itself is never sent — only the sign — and no manifest, message, or check-in is included.

Luna's dream interpretations are written the same way, under the same permission, and only when you ask her to read a specific dream. Writing a dream down and saving it sends it nowhere. When you do ask her to read one, we send the text of that single dream, the calendar date it was written on, your app language, and the Luna persona you chose. No other dream, and no manifest, message, or check-in, is included.

Luna's fortune cookies are written the same way, under the same permission. Breaking one sends the least of anything in this section: the calendar date the fortune is for, your app language, which of the day's cookies you are opening, and the Luna persona you chose. Nothing you have written is included — no manifest, message, check-in, or dream — and unlike a crystal ball reading, not even your zodiac sign. The fortune Luna writes back is kept on your device for the rest of that day and is never uploaded.

Your name and email address are not included in what we send to OpenAI. Requests are authenticated so we can enforce abuse limits and linked-account daily allowances. Our server also sends OpenAI a one-way, pseudonymous hash derived from your account identifier for abuse prevention; it is not included in Luna's prompt and cannot be used by OpenAI to recover your Firebase account identifier.

Your choice and legal basis. For the AI processing described in this section, including any sensitive information you choose to put in free text, Manifestle relies on your explicit consent. Choosing Allow Luna to Reply applies only to the Luna AI features listed above. Refusing or withdrawing consent leaves the rest of Manifestle available. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal; limited records may still be retained where necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and the 30-day periods explained below.

Training and retention. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer explicitly opts in. Manifestle sets each request not to be stored as Responses API application state; OpenAI may nevertheless retain abuse-monitoring logs containing prompts and responses for up to 30 days by default. See OpenAI's API data controls. On our own servers, the text of a Luna reply, reading, dream interpretation, or fortune is stored briefly so that a retried request cannot be charged or answered twice; these records are automatically deleted after 30 days, and immediately when you delete your account. Luna Memory is different from those short-lived request records: it remains until you clear it in Settings or delete your account.

Luna is a companion, not a therapist, doctor, or emergency service. If a message suggests immediate risk of harm, the app responds with crisis resources instead of a companion reply.

5. Shared Manifest and other people

Shared Manifest lets you share a journey with a friend. Anything you post there — your journey title, daily entries, and notes — is visible to the member you share it with. Do not put anything in a shared journey that you would not want that person to read.

The zodiac sign derived from your birth date is visible to accepted Shared Manifest friends. Your birth date is not.

Every shared journey offers a Report control for content that is abusive or otherwise objectionable, alongside the ability to remove the friend. You can also block a person. A block removes the connection and stops a new invite, friendship, or shared entry between you in either direction. Unblocking does not restore the friendship. Reports stay available after a block. Reports reach us at support@tortubuild.com and we act on them, including removing content and terminating accounts.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers. The app does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track you. The Android app does send crash diagnostics (section 7) — a crash report is not analytics, and it carries nothing you wrote.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not use your journal content for advertising or profiling.

7. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check) — account sign-in, cloud storage of the data listed in section 4, notifications, and abuse prevention.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Android only. When the app crashes or hits an error it can recover from, it sends a diagnostic report: where in the code it happened, the app version and whether it was a debug or release build, the device model and Android version, and an identifier Crashlytics generates for the installation. It does not send your manifests, messages, check-ins, dreams, birth date, name, email, or account id. The iOS app has no crash reporting at all.
  • OpenAI API — generating Luna's replies and updating Luna Memory, as described in section 3.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and App Store purchases.
  • Google Play — Android only. Distribution of the app and processing of Manifestle Plus purchases. Google Play tells the app whether a subscription is currently active; your payment details are handled by Google and never reach us. Sign in with Google is handled by Firebase Authentication, above.

8. Legal bases and international processing

Manifestle uses the following legal bases, where the relevant privacy law recognizes them:

  • Consent for sending the section 3 data to OpenAI and generating the AI feature you request.
  • Performance of our agreement with you for providing the app, authenticating an account, syncing the cloud data you choose to keep, restoring it, and processing purchases.
  • Legitimate interests for proportionate security, fraud and abuse prevention, enforcing daily allowances, reliable request handling, and responding to content reports. These interests do not permit us to use your private writing for advertising or profiling.
  • Legal obligations and legal claims where we must keep or disclose limited information under applicable law.

Firebase, OpenAI, Apple, and Google may process personal data outside your country, including outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. OpenAI processes API customer data on our behalf under its Data Processing Addendum. For transfers covered by European or UK data-protection law, that addendum incorporates the applicable Standard Contractual Clauses or UK addendum. Transfers from Türkiye are also subject to Article 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law and its required transfer safeguards. Your in-app AI choice does not waive those rights or replace any contractual or regulatory safeguard that the law requires.

You may ask us for information about the safeguards relevant to you at privacy@tortubuild.com.

9. Deleting your data

Settings → Your Data → Delete Manifestle Account deletes your account and the data stored with it: your cloud manifests and conversations, daily check-ins, your dream journal and Luna's interpretations of those dreams, Luna profile state, your birth date and zodiac sign, username reservation, avatar, persona, friend invitations and friendships, your entries in shared journeys, your notification tokens, your linked-account daily allowance markers, and your Luna chat records, including Luna Memory. Deletion is permanent once completed; there is no recovery period or archived copy of your journey.

It also clears everything the app holds about you on that device — your manifests and conversations, check-ins, dreams and their interpretations, the readings and fortunes Luna wrote for you, Luna progress, birth date and zodiac sign, avatar and persona, preferences, the app's offline cache of your cloud data, and your AI permission — and removes your sign-in after cloud deletion succeeds. If deletion fails or is interrupted, the app keeps your local journey and tells you to retry. Some cloud records may already have been removed; retrying finishes the remaining cleanup.

Starting again afterwards starts you from nothing: a new account is a new account, and no manifest, profile picture, birth date, preference, or progress from the deleted one comes back — except for the limited safety and allowance records described next.

What the device keeps. Manifestle gives each device a small free allowance every day: how many messages Luna will answer, how many crystal ball readings she will give, how many dreams she will read, and how many fortune cookies you may open. The device keeps its local count of what has already been used today when you log out, delete an account, or create a new one, and that local count resets when the next day begins. For a linked account, the server also keeps that day's Crystal Ball, dream-interpretation, and fortune-cookie usage markers so the account's allowances cannot be refilled by reinstalling or signing in elsewhere. Those account markers contain nothing you wrote and nothing Luna said, are never shown to anyone, and are deleted with the account. We keep the device's local same-day count after account deletion so deleting one account and making another on the same device cannot refill the day's free allowance. If a birth-date answer previously indicated an age below 13, the device also retains that restriction across ordinary date edits and account deletion; it does not retain the rejected birth date for this purpose.

During account deletion, we create a temporary server record containing the account identifier and an expiry time, without your writing. It blocks in-flight requests from recreating deleted data. The record expires after two hours and is removed by the database's automatic retention process, which may run later.

What we keep. If you reported someone's content to us, that report — including the fact that you filed it — is kept after your account is deleted, because we need it to act on abuse and to show we did. Anything a friend independently holds, such as a shared journey you both took part in, stays with their account; your entries and your membership are removed from it.

Deleting the app alone does not delete the cloud copy, because your sign-in survives on the device. Use Delete Manifestle Account, or write to privacy@tortubuild.com and we will delete it for you.

You can also export your manifests and reflections at any time from Settings → Your Data → Export My Data.

10. Children

Manifestle is not directed to children under 13. If a birth date shows that the person is under 13, Manifestle does not create or link an account from that answer, does not send their information to OpenAI, and does not open Shared Manifest. The date itself is not kept on the device just to remember that result. An account that already exists is not deleted automatically and is not given a different birth date; you can delete it from Settings or by writing to us. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. The in-app export and delete controls cover most of these directly. For anything else, write to privacy@tortubuild.com.
