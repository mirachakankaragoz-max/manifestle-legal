Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Summary

Manifestle is a local-first personal ritual app. Your private sealed intentions, private chat history with Luna, private daily check-ins, Luna evolution/customization state, reminder preferences, and widget snapshot stay on your device. We do not upload those private ritual records to our cloud for storage or multi-device sync.

Limited cloud processing happens only for: (1) optional Shared Manifest collaboration, (2) account authentication and collaboration identity (including optional username), and (3) Luna chat messages you send for AI replies through our secure backend proxy.

Services we use

  • On device: SwiftData (app group container), UserDefaults, local notifications, WidgetKit snapshot storage, Apple StoreKit.
  • Cloud (limited): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service (for linked-account push registration), and Manifestle’s server-side Luna chat proxy (lunaChat), which calls Google Gemini with the API key kept on the server.

Data stored only on your device (not uploaded as private cloud storage)

The following remain on your iPhone (app-group / app storage) and are not written to Firestore as your private journal:

  • Sealed intentions / private manifests
  • Private Luna chat history saved in the app
  • Private daily Luna check-ins (mood, notes, local replies)
  • Luna progression, prompt-usage counters, recovery flags, and customization selections
  • Local reminder schedule preferences
  • Daily Luna widget snapshot shared only between the app and its widget extension

You may export a local JSON snapshot of your data from Settings; export stays under your control (share sheet / files).

Data processed in the cloud

1. Authentication and collaboration identity

  • The app may start an anonymous Firebase Auth session so secure features (including chat) can use an app-level user ID without a launch-time login wall.
  • If you link Apple or Google, we process the identity tokens Apple/Google provide and may store: Firebase user ID, auth provider, whether Apple is linked, display name (if provided by the provider), and—if you choose one—an app username reserved for collaboration features.
  • A lightweight profile document may be stored under users/{your uid} for these fields (and a coarse Luna stage label used for social/collaboration context). This is not a full copy of your private manifests, private chat, or private daily check-ins.
  • Usernames are reserved in a dedicated username registry so they remain unique.

2. Optional Shared Manifest (friend collaboration)

Only if you use Shared Manifest / friend features:

  • Friend invite codes and friendship records
  • Shared journey metadata (dates, shared intention text, members)
  • Shared daily entries you submit (completed or rest, optional short note ≤ 200 characters)
  • Photo support is metadata-only today (no image bytes uploaded)

Shared-manifest content is visible to the participants in that shared journey. We do not sell personal data.

3. Luna chat via secure backend proxy

When you chat with Luna, the app sends to Manifestle’s backend proxy (not a client-side Gemini key):

  • Your latest message
  • The sealed intention context for that conversation
  • A bounded recent message history
  • App language preference
  • Authentication and App Check tokens to protect the service

The proxy forwards content to Google Gemini to generate Luna’s reply. Gemini’s API key is kept on the server.

For reliability, abuse prevention, and duplicate-request handling, the backend may temporarily store chat request bookkeeping (including completed reply text and daily usage counters) associated with your auth user ID. This is not the same as cloud-backing your entire private journal; your full conversation remains saved locally on device.

4. Notifications

  • Daily check-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • If you link a non-anonymous account and allow remote notifications, a device push token (FCM) may be stored under your user record so we can address notifications to that device.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Manifestle does not receive or store your full payment-card information. Entitlements are verified on device via StoreKit.

How we use information

  • Operate the local ritual experience you see in the app
  • Authenticate sessions and protect the chat API (App Check + signed-in user)
  • Generate Luna replies when you choose to chat
  • Power optional friend / Shared Manifest collaboration
  • Enforce service integrity (rate limits / daily chat reservations)
  • Process account deletion when you request it

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use private local ritual data for advertising networks.

Data sharing and disclosure

  • Google Gemini (via our proxy) processes chat content you send in order to generate replies.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud infrastructure hosts Auth, Firestore, and related services we configure for account, Shared Manifest, push tokens, and chat bookkeeping.
  • Apple processes Sign in with Apple (if used) and StoreKit purchases.
  • Shared Manifest participants see content you share in that journey.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

Data retention and deletion

On device: Local data remains until you delete it in-app, delete the app, or use system storage controls.

In-app deletion: Settings provides Delete Account & Sign Out, which:

  1. Requests deletion of associated Manifestle cloud data (Auth user, profile, username reservation where applicable, friend/shared-manifest membership data, chat request/usage bookkeeping, push tokens) via our backend; then
  2. Clears this device’s local Manifestle data (private manifests/chat/check-ins, Luna profile, reminders, widget snapshot) and returns you to the welcome flow.

App Store purchase history is managed by Apple and is not deleted by Manifestle.

You may also contact us at the email below for assistance with deletion requests if needed.

Retention: Cloud account, collaboration, and chat bookkeeping data are retained only as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or until deleted as above. Specific chat bookkeeping retention windows may be shortened over time as the production AI policy is finalized; account deletion removes the chat bookkeeping records associated with your uid.

Your choices and controls

  • Use Manifestle without linking Apple/Google (guest/anonymous session) for the core local ritual
  • Link an account only when you want collaboration features
  • Choose a username only when required for friend/shared-manifest flows
  • Use or avoid Shared Manifest and Luna chat
  • Export a local data snapshot
  • Delete account & clear this device in Settings
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple account settings
  • Control notification permission in iOS Settings

Children

Manifestle is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

International transfers

Cloud services we use may process data in regions where the provider operates (including outside your country). Transfers are handled under those providers’ terms and applicable law.

Health and professional advice

Manifestle is a personal reflection companion, not emergency, medical, legal, financial, or mental-health care. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published. Continued use after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.
