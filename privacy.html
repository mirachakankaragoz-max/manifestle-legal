Manifestle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Manifestle is a private ritual app: you seal intentions, talk with Luna, record daily check-ins, keep a dream journal, and open your Moon Locket. This policy describes exactly what stays on your device, what is sent elsewhere, when, and why. Where this policy and the app disagree, treat it as a bug and tell us at privacy@tortubuild.com.

Manifestle is operated by TortuBuild ("we", "us"), which is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact the controller at privacy@tortubuild.com.

1. The short version

  • Without a linked account, your journey is stored locally rather than backed up to your Manifestle account. Optional AI processing and the temporary server records described in section 3 are separate from that backup.
  • Luna's replies are generated by AI. They require your explicit in-app permission. Until you grant it, nothing you write is sent to OpenAI for Luna's AI features.
  • If you link an Apple or Google account, your manifests (including their conversations with Luna), daily check-ins, dream journal, and Luna profile settings are stored in our cloud so they can be restored on a new or reinstalled device.
  • Luna Memory is a Manifestle Plus feature for linked accounts. It keeps a compact set of useful details from your conversations. You can view or clear it in Settings.
  • For a linked account, we also store the current day's used Crystal Ball, dream interpretation, and Moon Locket allowances. These usage markers contain no text you wrote and no horoscope, dream interpretation, or Moon Locket message Luna generated. They prevent today's allowances from being restored by logging out, reinstalling, or signing in elsewhere.
  • The Dream Journal needs an account. Saved dreams are backed up privately with your linked journey. A dream is sent to OpenAI only when you ask Luna to read that particular one and have granted AI permission.
  • Moon Locket needs an account too, and sends nothing you have written. Opening your locket asks Luna for a message; the request carries the date, your app language, whether you are opening the daily or bonus message, and the Luna persona you chose. It does not include anything you wrote, and it does not include your zodiac sign.
  • Some versions include optional Meta and TikTok advertising-measurement SDKs. They remain inactive unless measurement is available in that version and you grant the separate advertising permission described in section 6. Your private writing is not an advertising payload.
  • You can delete your account and its journey data from inside the app. Section 9 explains the limited security, moderation, and device records that remain.

2. Data stored only on your device

Before you link an account, and for as long as you remain a guest ("wanderer"), Manifestle stores your journey locally rather than syncing it to a linked account:

  • Your sealed manifests and their text.
  • Your conversations with Luna.
  • Your daily check-ins, moods, and notes.
  • Your Luna companion progress, appearance choices, reminder time, appearance and language preferences.
  • The birth date and zodiac sign you choose when you first open the app.
  • While you remain a guest, how much of today's free allowance you have used — the messages Luna has answered. These local product-allowance counts are kept on that device, are not uploaded, and reset each day. If you link an account, the limited charm usage described in section 4 is also stored under that account.

This is a storage distinction, not a promise that nothing is sent from a guest session. With your AI permission, the selected conversation data goes to our server and OpenAI as described in section 3. Firebase anonymous authentication, App Check, and server request/abuse records also operate for guest AI requests. Crystal Ball, the Dream Journal, and Moon Locket require a linked account.

Deleting the app removes this local data from the device. Note that a linked account's copy in our cloud is not deleted by removing the app — see section 9. On iOS, a separate system backup may retain a copy as described below.

Your phone's own backup. Manifestle disables Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer for all local app data, so Android does not copy the information listed above into your Google backup or transfer it to a replacement phone. On iOS, an iCloud or computer backup may include local app data and may put it back when you restore your phone. iOS backups are made by Apple under your Apple account and are governed by Apple's terms rather than by this policy; we cannot read them and we are not sent a copy.

Because Android backup is disabled, a guest journey cannot be recovered after the app is uninstalled or the phone is lost or replaced. A linked account can restore the Local Journey data described in section 4 from Manifestle's cloud.

3. Luna's AI replies

Luna's replies are written using the OpenAI API. Manifestle asks for your explicit permission the first time an AI feature would send data to OpenAI. Until you grant it, Manifestle does not send any of the data described in this section to OpenAI. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Luna's AI Replies; the app stops new AI requests immediately.

When permission is granted, each reply sends the following to our own server, which relays it to the OpenAI API:

  • The message you just wrote.
  • An excerpt of the intention Luna is holding for that conversation.
  • Up to the last 10 messages of that conversation, with per-message length limits.
  • For a linked account with active Manifestle Plus, Luna's compact memory from earlier conversations, if one exists.
  • Your app language and the Luna persona you chose.

Luna Memory. For a linked account with active Manifestle Plus, our server may periodically ask OpenAI to update a compact memory using the recent conversation. It is limited to useful details such as preferences, important people, goals, routines, and milestones. We instruct the model not to infer or retain sensitive traits, credentials, precise location, financial details, diagnoses, or transient moods. The resulting memory is stored privately in Firebase under your account, is included only in future Luna chats while Plus is active, and is never visible to Shared Manifest friends. If Plus expires, the memory is paused but remains available for you to view or clear in Settings → Luna's Memory.

Luna's horoscopes from the crystal ball are written the same way, under the same permission. Opening a horoscope sends only your zodiac sign, whether the horoscope is daily or weekly, the calendar date it is for, your app language, and the Luna persona you chose. Your birth date itself is never sent — only the sign — and no manifest, message, or check-in is included.

Luna's dream interpretations are written the same way, under the same permission, and only when you ask her to read a specific dream. Saving a dream backs it up to your linked account but does not by itself send it to OpenAI. When you ask her to read one, we send the text of that single dream, the calendar date it was written on, your app language, and the Luna persona you chose. No other dream, and no manifest, message, or check-in, is included. The dream text is first assessed by OpenAI for safety; when interpretation is appropriate, a further OpenAI request generates it. The app may instead provide clarification or support guidance. This assessment is automated and may make mistakes.

Luna's Moon Locket messages are written the same way, under the same permission. Opening your locket sends the least of anything in this section: the calendar date the message is for, your app language, whether you are opening the daily or bonus message, and the Luna persona you chose. Nothing you have written is included — no manifest, message, check-in, or dream — and unlike a horoscope from the crystal ball, not even your zodiac sign. The message Luna writes back is kept locally for that day and in a temporary server retry/recovery record described below; it is not synced as part of your journey backup.

We do not automatically add your account name, email address, or saved birth date to OpenAI requests. Identifying details you choose to write in a message, intention, or dream, or that appear in permitted Luna Memory, can be included in the processing above. Requests are authenticated so we can enforce abuse limits and linked-account daily allowances. Our server also sends OpenAI a one-way, pseudonymous hash derived from your account identifier for abuse prevention; it is not included in Luna's prompt and cannot be used by OpenAI to recover your Firebase account identifier.

Your choice and legal basis. For the AI processing described in this section, including any sensitive information you choose to put in free text, Manifestle relies on your explicit consent. Choosing Allow Luna to Reply applies only to the Luna AI features listed above. Refusing or withdrawing consent leaves the rest of Manifestle available. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal; limited records may still be retained where necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and the 30-day periods explained below.

Training and retention. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer explicitly opts in. Manifestle sets each request not to be stored as Responses API application state; OpenAI may nevertheless retain abuse-monitoring logs containing prompts and responses for up to 30 days by default, or longer when required by law or necessary for safety and abuse prevention under its policies. See OpenAI's API data controls. On our own servers, the text of a Luna reply, horoscope, dream interpretation, or Moon Locket message is stored so that a retried request can recover the answer without generating or using an allowance again. These request records are marked to expire after 30 days; automatic database deletion runs asynchronously and may complete later. They are also removed when account deletion succeeds. Request counts, timestamps, and abuse-control records are separate from the saved reply text. Luna Memory is different from those short-lived request records: it remains until you clear it in Settings or delete your account.

Luna is a companion, not a therapist, doctor, or emergency service. The app may provide safety guidance instead of an ordinary reply when it detects possible harm, but it cannot reliably detect every risk, monitor emergencies, or contact emergency services for you.

5. Shared Manifest and other people

Shared Manifest lets you share a journey with a friend. Anything you post there — your journey title, daily entries, and notes — is visible to the member you share it with. Do not put anything in a shared journey that you would not want that person to read.

The zodiac sign derived from your birth date is visible to accepted Shared Manifest friends. Your birth date is not.

Every shared journey offers a Report control for content that is abusive or otherwise objectionable, alongside the ability to remove the friend. You can also block a person. A block removes the connection and stops a new invite, friendship, or shared entry between you in either direction. Unblocking does not restore the friendship. Reports stay available after a block. Reports reach us at support@tortubuild.com and we act on them, including removing content and terminating accounts.

6. Optional advertising measurement and limits

Current availability. Meta and TikTok advertising measurement is currently disabled in Manifestle. Including an SDK in a build does not activate it. The following explains the optional processing in versions where measurement is enabled.

Where available, Advertising measurement lets you choose whether Manifestle sends limited app events to Meta and TikTok to measure our campaigns and help those platforms find people interested in Manifestle. Each provider must also be enabled and configured in that version. The choice starts off. It is separate from Luna's AI permission, and declining it does not restrict app features. Versions without enabled measurement do not initialize these advertising SDKs.

The events describe an app installation or opening, a successfully sealed intention, the first check-in, viewing the Plus screen, or an initial Plus purchase. Purchase events may include the product identifier, price and currency; they do not include payment-card information. SDKs may also transmit event times, app/SDK and operating-system versions, device and installation identifiers, IP/network information, locale/time-zone information and SDK diagnostics. Providers can also infer a general region from network or locale information; Manifestle does not request precise device location for this measurement. These records are not necessarily anonymous: the platforms may link them to information they hold for advertising measurement and optimization. We do not provide your Firebase account ID, name, email, birth date, mood, intention text, chat, notes, dream text or generated readings as advertising-event parameters.

Manifestle disables automatic page-content and form-field collection for advertising. The TikTok SDK used by Manifestle is a reviewed local variant with its screen-image capture, storage and upload mechanism removed. Advertising events do not contain screenshots, your private text, or recordings from your microphone or camera.

iOS. Direct event sharing with these platforms additionally requires permission in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. TikTok's SDK may access the advertising identifier when that system permission is granted; Manifestle disables Meta's IDFA collection. If you grant Manifestle's advertising permission but decline Apple's tracking prompt, Manifestle uses only Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork conversion reporting for this measurement. Apple's postbacks are handled through its attribution system rather than a user-level event stream from Manifestle to an advertising platform.

Android. Manifestle disables advertising-ID collection in both SDK configurations and removes their advertising-ID permissions. Limited device/installation identifiers and app-event information can still be shared after your advertising permission; disabling the advertising ID does not make that sharing anonymous.

Where the feature is available, you can withdraw this permission in Settings → Advertising measurement. The app stops new business-event submissions and disables TikTok event transmission; requests already submitted cannot be recalled. SDKs may retain pending records for delivery and the providers may retain data already received under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Deleting a Manifestle account does not automatically erase records already held independently by an advertising platform. Contact privacy@tortubuild.com for help exercising your data rights. We do not promise a fixed retention period for data controlled by those providers.

  • We do not rent personal data or sell it for money. Optional advertising data sharing is described above; applicable privacy laws may treat advertising disclosures as sharing.
  • We do not use your journal content for advertising or profiling.

  • Firebase Crashlytics — Android only. The iOS app does not use Firebase Crashlytics; enabled advertising SDKs may send their own SDK diagnostics as described in section 6.
  • Meta and TikTok — optional advertising measurement described in section 6, governed by their applicable business-product terms and privacy policies: Meta Privacy Policy and TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI API — generating Luna's replies and updating Luna Memory, as described in section 3.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and App Store purchases on iOS.
  • Google Play — Android only. Distribution of the app and processing of Manifestle Plus and permanent appearance purchases. Google Play provides product, purchase, and entitlement information to the app; payment-card details are handled by Google and never reach us. Sign in with Google is handled by Firebase Authentication, above.

8. Legal bases and international processing

Manifestle uses the following legal bases, where the relevant privacy law recognizes them:

  • Consent for sending the section 3 data to OpenAI and generating the AI feature you request.
  • Separate advertising consent for the optional section 6 measurement, with additional system permission on iOS. Neither advertising nor AI permission is a condition of using the other features.
  • Performance of our agreement with you for providing the app, authenticating an account, syncing the cloud data you choose to keep, restoring it, and processing purchases.
  • Legitimate interests for proportionate security, fraud and abuse prevention, enforcing daily allowances, reliable request handling, and responding to content reports. These interests do not permit us to use your private writing for advertising or profiling.
  • Legal obligations and legal claims where we must keep or disclose limited information under applicable law.

Firebase, OpenAI, Apple, Google, and, when advertising measurement is enabled, Meta and TikTok may process personal data outside your country, including outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. OpenAI processes API customer data on our behalf under its Data Processing Addendum. For transfers covered by European or UK data-protection law, that addendum incorporates the applicable Standard Contractual Clauses or UK addendum. Transfers from Türkiye are also subject to Article 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law and its required transfer safeguards. Your in-app AI or advertising choice does not waive those rights or replace any contractual or regulatory safeguard that the law requires.

You may ask us for information about the safeguards relevant to you at privacy@tortubuild.com.

9. Deleting your data

Settings → Your Data → Delete Manifestle Account deletes your account and the data stored with it: your cloud manifests and conversations, daily check-ins, your dream journal and Luna's interpretations of those dreams, Luna profile state, your birth date and zodiac sign, username reservation, avatar, persona, friend invitations and friendships, your entries in shared journeys, your notification tokens, your linked-account daily allowance markers, and your Luna chat records, including Luna Memory. Deletion is permanent once completed; there is no recovery period or archived copy of your journey.

It also clears everything the app holds about you on that device — your manifests and conversations, check-ins, dreams and their interpretations, the horoscopes and Moon Locket messages Luna wrote for you, Luna progress, birth date and zodiac sign, avatar and persona, preferences, the app's offline cache of your cloud data, and your AI permission — and removes your sign-in after cloud deletion succeeds. If deletion fails or is interrupted, the app keeps your local journey and tells you to retry. Some cloud records may already have been removed. Retry the deletion; if you can no longer sign in or the error continues, contact privacy@tortubuild.com so we can complete the cleanup.

Starting again afterwards starts you from nothing: a new account is a new account, and no manifest, profile picture, birth date, preference, or progress from the deleted one comes back — except for the limited safety and allowance records described next.

What the device keeps. Manifestle gives each device a small free allowance every day: how many messages Luna will answer, how many horoscopes she will prepare in the crystal ball, how many dreams she will read, and how many Moon Locket messages you may open. The device keeps its local count of what has already been used today when you log out, delete an account, or create a new one, and that local count resets when the next day begins. For a linked account, the server also keeps that day's Crystal Ball, dream-interpretation, and Moon Locket usage markers so the account's allowances cannot be refilled by reinstalling or signing in elsewhere. Those account markers contain nothing you wrote and nothing Luna said, are never shown to anyone, and are deleted with the account. We keep the device's local same-day count after account deletion so deleting one account and making another on the same device cannot refill the day's free allowance. If a birth-date answer previously indicated an age below 13, the device also retains that restriction across ordinary date edits and account deletion; it does not retain the rejected birth date for this purpose.

When account deletion starts, we create a small security record associated with the account identifier, containing deletion status and timing information, without your writing. It blocks writes from existing sessions and in-flight requests from recreating deleted data. While cleanup is incomplete, the record has no automatic expiry and remains until deletion can be retried or completed through support. Only after account-data cleanup and deletion of the sign-in account succeed do we set the record to expire two hours later. Firestore then removes it automatically through its time-to-live (TTL) process. Removal is asynchronous, usually within 24 hours after expiry; this is not a promise of deletion at an exact time.

Small installation-only flags may also remain, including whether a TikTok installation event has already been recorded in an enabled version. These prevent duplicate installation events; they do not contain your writing or restore the deleted journey. Advertising permission is cleared when you leave the account.

What we keep. If you reported someone's content to us, that report — including the fact that you filed it — is kept after your account is deleted, because we need it to act on abuse and to show we did. Anything a friend independently holds, such as a shared journey you both took part in, stays with their account; your entries and your membership are removed from it.

Deleting the app alone does not request deletion of your cloud account or its data. Use Delete Manifestle Account, or write to privacy@tortubuild.com and we will delete it for you.

You can also export your manifests and reflections at any time from Settings → Your Data → Export My Data.

10. Children

Manifestle is not directed to children under 13. If a birth date shows that the person is under 13, Manifestle does not create or link an account from that answer, does not send their information to OpenAI, and does not open Shared Manifest. The date itself is not kept on the device just to remember that result. An account that already exists is not deleted automatically and is not given a different birth date; you can delete it from Settings or by writing to us. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. The in-app export and delete controls cover most of these directly. For anything else, write to privacy@tortubuild.com.
